How to use this
  1. Pick the award and classification that matches the worker.
  2. Choose Permanent/FT or Casual — this sets the Full Time Rate or Casual Rate (+25%) used below.
  3. Pick the state (payroll tax differs by state) and a method: $ Margin (set a flat dollar profit) or Multiple (set a multiple of the rate, margin is whatever's left over).
  4. The Charge to client figure is what you'd quote — Cost to agency is what it actually costs Fuse to employ them.
  5. Fill in the client name to get a full quote you can copy.

How to use this
  1. Use this when you want to check whether a worker is being paid enough — for example, a client tells you a casual's hourly rate and you want to make sure it's legal.
  2. Select the award, classification and employment type that matches the worker.
  3. Enter what they're actually being paid per hour, and how many hours the check should cover.
  4. Tap Run test. PASS means the rate is at or above the legal minimum. FAIL means it's below — you'll see exactly how much they're being underpaid, in total.
